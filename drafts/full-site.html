What we build

  • Web applications

    Customer portals, booking and ordering systems, dashboards: software your customers and staff use in the browser, on any device.

  • Mobile apps

    Apps for iPhone and Android, built to work with the rest of your systems rather than beside them.

  • Internal tools

    Replace the spreadsheets, copy-pasting and manual steps that take up your team’s week.

  • Integrations and automation

    Connect the tools you already use, such as accounting, your e-shop, CRM and payments, so data moves without anyone retyping it.

How we work

  1. Talk

    We learn how your business runs and where software could help. Sometimes the answer is a tool that already exists, and we’ll say so.

  2. Plan

    You get a written plan: what we’ll build, what it will cost and roughly how long it will take.

  3. Build

    We show you working software every week or two, so you can try it and steer while it takes shape.

  4. Launch and support

    We put it live, help your team get started and stay on to maintain and improve it.

Why a new studio

Primivant was founded in 2026. Working with a small, new studio means:

  • You talk directly to the people designing and building your software.
  • Your project gets our full attention, not the time left over from bigger clients.
  • We explain things in plain language and tell you when something isn’t worth building.
